I'm a computational cognitive neuroscientist interested in how people adapt their learning, memory and decision-making to the environments they experience. 

**I am very excited to be joining the [Columbia Psychology department](https://psychology.columbia.edu/) in summer 2027 as an Assistant Professor**. Please [get in touch](mailto:eleanor.holton@princeton.edu) if you're interested in joining my future lab in Autumn 2027 as a lab manager, PhD student, or postdoc! 

## Research

My research investigates mechanisms of goal-directed behavior in humans, combining computational modelling, neuroimaging, and naturalistic behavioral paradigms. I am interested in the inherent cognitive trade-offs we face, for example [between goal persistence and flexibility](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/fulltext/S1364-6613(25)00278-5), or between knowledge structures that [facilitate generalisation versus avoid interference](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-025-02318-y). I aim to understand how individual differences in balancing these trade-offs shape behaviour, and how disruption to these processes contribute to psychiatric and neurological symptoms.
